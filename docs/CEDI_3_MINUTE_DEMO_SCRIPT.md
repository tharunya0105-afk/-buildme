# BuildMe — 3-Minute CEDI Demo Script

**Total time**: 3 minutes
**Goal**: Make the evaluator understand BuildMe's value, honesty, and next steps.

---

## 0:00–0:30 — THE PROBLEM (30 seconds)

**Say**:

> "Construction cost estimation in India is fragmented. Engineers use government benchmarks that don't reflect local prices. Contractor quotations vary 40-60% for similar scope. Material prices change quarterly but estimates are static. The result: budget overruns, client disputes, and no way to verify whether a quotation is reasonable."

**Show**: Nothing yet. Just state the problem clearly.

---

## 0:30–1:00 — THE ESTIMATE (30 seconds)

**Say**:

> "BuildMe connects these dots. I'll enter a real project: Coimbatore, 1800 square feet, 2 floors, standard quality."

**Do**: Open the CEDI Demo page, enter the parameters, click Generate.

**Say**:

> "BuildMe produces a planning range — low, central, high — based on real government data. This isn't a guess. Every number traces to a source."

**Show**: The estimate result with planning range.

---

## 1:00–1:30 — THE METHODOLOGY (30 seconds)

**Say**:

> "Here's how BuildMe calculated this. Layer 1: CPWD benchmark — the official government rate. Layer 2: BCCI time adjustment — tracking how construction costs changed from 2019 to now. Layer 3: Location adjustment — Coimbatore's index versus Chennai's reference. Layer 4: Quality adjustment for standard specification."

**Do**: Click "Show Source Provenance" on the demo page.

**Say**:

> "Every number has a source. No black boxes."

**Show**: The source provenance list.

---

## 1:30–2:00 — MARKET EVIDENCE (30 seconds)

**Say**:

> "BuildMe also structures real contractor quotations. Here are three actual quotations from Tamil Nadu and Kerala. I can compare them against the BuildMe estimate."

**Do**: Navigate to the quotation comparison section.

**Say**:

> "This is a market comparison — not accuracy validation. A quotation is market evidence, not the final cost. But it shows how market prices relate to government benchmarks."

**Show**: Quotation comparison.

**Important**: Say explicitly:

> "I'm not claiming BuildMe is accurate. I'm claiming it provides a transparent reference point."

---

## 2:00–2:30 — SPATIAL + TRACKING (30 seconds)

**Say**:

> "BuildMe also has spatial intelligence — BCCI data for 16 Tamil Nadu centres with quarterly tracking. And project tracking — estimate → expenses → evidence → completion → validation."

**Do**: Show the validation dashboard with honest empty states.

**Say**:

> "Currently: 0 completed projects, 0 ground-truth observations. The infrastructure exists. What's needed is real-world testing."

**Show**: The validation dashboard.

---

## 2:30–3:00 — THE ASK (30 seconds)

**Say**:

> "BuildMe is a working prototype on real government data and market evidence. I'm not claiming accuracy because I don't have the evidence yet. The next step is to validate BuildMe on real construction projects with CEDI's support."

**Show**: The "What We've Proven" and "What We Haven't Proven" sections.

**End with**:

> "We haven't claimed accuracy yet because we don't have enough completed-project ground truth. The next step is to validate BuildMe on real construction projects."

**This sentence is a strength, not a weakness.**

---

## DEMO RULES

1. **Never claim accuracy** — you don't have the evidence
2. **Never call it AI** — it's rule-based
3. **Never show fabricated data** — only real data
4. **Always show the "not proven" section** — honesty builds credibility
5. **Always end with the ask** — what you need from CEDI

---

## IF ASKED: "How accurate is it?"

**Answer**: "I don't know yet. We have 0 completed projects to compare against. The estimate is transparent and reproducible, but accuracy requires real-world validation. That's what the pilot is for."

---

## IF ASKED: "Where is the AI?"

**Answer**: "BuildMe's current intelligence is rule-based, not AI. An AI document extraction pipeline exists but requires an API key we don't have yet. We're not claiming AI capabilities we don't have."

---

## IF ASKED: "How many users?"

**Answer**: "Zero. The product exists but hasn't been used by anyone other than me. We're currently recruiting civil engineers for the first pilot."

---

## IF ASKED: "How will you make money?"

**Answer**: "We have pricing hypotheses — subscription or per-project. All are unvalidated. The first step is to determine whether engineers find BuildMe valuable enough to use, then test willingness to pay."

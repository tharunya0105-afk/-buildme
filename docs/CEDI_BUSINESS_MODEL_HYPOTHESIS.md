# BuildMe — Business Model Hypothesis

**Status**: ALL HYPOTHESES — NOT VALIDATED

---

## Customer Hypothesis

### Primary Customer: Civil Engineers

**Why**: They prepare estimates, compare quotations, and track projects. They experience the pain most directly.

**Evidence**: Father is a civil engineer. Direct observation of estimation workflow.

**Validation needed**: Would they actually use BuildMe? Would they pay?

### Secondary Customer: Small Contractors

**Why**: They prepare quotations and track costs. They benefit from better benchmarking.

**Evidence**: Quotation data shows significant variance between contractors.

**Validation needed**: Would contractors adopt a tool that might expose their pricing?

### Tertiary Customer: Homeowners

**Why**: They want transparency in construction costs. They currently have no way to verify quotations.

**Evidence**: Homeowner portal exists in BuildMe.

**Validation needed**: Would homeowners trust a tool built by an engineer's son?

---

## Problem Hypothesis

### Severe Problem
> "Construction cost estimation is fragmented, inaccurate, and opaque, leading to budget overruns and client disputes."

**Evidence**: 9 real quotations show 40-60% variance for similar scope. Government benchmarks don't reflect local prices.

**Validation needed**: Do engineers actually experience this as a severe problem? Or is it "manageable"?

### Willingness to Pay
> "Engineers would pay ₹500-2000/month for a tool that improves estimation accuracy and reduces overruns."

**Evidence**: None. This is a guess.

**Validation needed**: Actual interviews and pricing experiments.

## What We Know vs What We Don't

### What We Know
1. Construction estimation is fragmented
2. Government data exists and can be structured
3. Market quotations can be compared transparently
4. A working prototype exists

### What We Don't Know
1. Will engineers actually use BuildMe?
2. Will they pay for it?
3. Does it actually improve estimation?
4. Is the problem severe enough to warrant a new tool?
5. Can BuildMe scale beyond a prototype?

---

## Honest Assessment

BuildMe currently has:
- A working product
- Real data
- A transparent methodology
- No users
- No revenue
- No validated business model

The business model is a hypothesis that requires real-world testing. The next step is not to build more features, but to discover whether anyone wants to use what already exists.

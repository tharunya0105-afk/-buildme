# BuildMe — Pilot Recruitment Plan

**Objective**: Recruit ONE genuine construction professional willing to test BuildMe on a real project.

---

## Ideal Pilot Profile

### Project Type
- Residential construction (new build or major renovation)
- Active or recently started (not completed)
- Location in Tamil Nadu (preferably Coimbatore or nearby)

### Participant
- Civil engineer or licensed surveyor
- Willing to share project parameters (area, floors, location, type)
- Willing to compare BuildMe estimate with their own
- Willing to track expenses during construction
- Willing to provide final cost when complete

### Project Characteristics
- Built-up area: 1,000–3,000 sqft
- Floors: 1–3
- Building type: RCC framed or load-bearing
- Quality: Standard or premium
- Estimated cost: ₹15L–₹1Cr

---

## Recruitment Channels

### Channel 1: Father's Network (Highest Priority)
Your father is a practicing civil engineer with direct access to:
- Other civil engineers
- Contractors
- Homeowners
- Construction consultants

**Action**: Ask your father to introduce you to one engineer who has an active residential project.

### Channel 2: Direct Outreach
Identify 3–5 civil engineers in Coimbatore through:
- Professional directories
- Construction industry contacts
- Local engineering associations

**Action**: Send a brief, honest introduction explaining the pilot.

### Channel 3: Referral
After completing 2–3 customer discovery interviews, ask for referrals.

---

## Pilot Offer (Non-Commercial)

### What You Offer
- Free use of BuildMe for one project
- A transparent planning estimate based on government benchmarks and market data
- Comparison with their existing estimate/quotation
- Expense tracking during construction
- Ground-truth analysis when complete

### What You Ask
- Permission to use project parameters (no personal information)
- Willingness to track expenses
- Final cost when complete (optional but valuable)
- Feedback on usefulness
- Permission to present anonymized results to CEDI

### What You Guarantee
- No confidential information published
- No personal names in presentations
- No financial information exposed
- Project data used only for validation analysis

## Pilot Timeline

### Week 1
- Conduct 5 customer discovery interviews
- Identify 2–3 potential pilot candidates
- Select best candidate

### Week 2
- Onboard pilot project
- Generate BuildMe estimate
- Compare with engineer's estimate
- Set up expense tracking

### Weeks 3–8
- Monitor project progress
- Record expenses as they occur
- Collect feedback
- Document any issues

### Week 8+
- Record final cost when complete
- Analyze BuildMe estimate vs actual
- Complete pilot report
- Prepare CEDI evidence

---

## Success Criteria

### Minimum Success
- One real project onboarded
- BuildMe estimate generated
- Engineer's own estimate/quotation compared
- At least 3 expenses recorded
- Engineer provides written feedback

### Ideal Success
- Project completed with final cost
- BuildMe estimate compared with actual
- Engineer recommends BuildMe to another engineer
- Quotation comparison reveals useful insights

---

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Engineer drops out
**Mitigation**: Have 2–3 backup candidates. Make onboarding simple.

### Risk: Project takes too long
**Mitigation**: Start with a project already in progress. Track existing expenses.

### Risk: Engineer won't share final cost
**Mitigation**: Make this optional. The estimate→quotation comparison is valuable even without completion.

### Risk: Engineer expects too much
**Mitigation**: Set clear expectations. "This is a research tool, not a finished product."

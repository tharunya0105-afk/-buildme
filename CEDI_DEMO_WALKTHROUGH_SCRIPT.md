# BuildMe — Product Demo Walkthrough Script
## For CEDI EiR Cohort 3 Application

Use this script to record a **3-5 minute screen recording** of BuildMe for your application.

---

## Setup Before Recording

1. Open browser to `http://localhost:57338`
2. Start screen recording (OBS, Loom, or Windows Game Bar: Win+G)
3. Set browser to full-screen (F11)
4. Clear any popups/notifications

---

## Script

### OPENING (15 seconds)

**Say:**
> "BuildMe is a construction decision-intelligence platform for independent civil engineers in Tamil Nadu. It connects what was planned, what was quoted, what changed, what was built, what was paid, and why the budget moved."

**Show:** Landing page — hover over "Know what your house will cost before you build it."

---

### STEP 1 — THE PROBLEM (20 seconds)

**Say:**
> "When a family builds a home in India, they typically get a lump-sum quotation. During construction, the budget increases. Nobody can explain why. BuildMe solves this."

**Show:** Point at the tagline and "Construction Intelligence Platform" badge.

---

### STEP 2 — LOG IN AS ENGINEER (10 seconds)

**Say:**
> "Let me log in as a civil engineer managing multiple construction sites."

**Show:** Click "I'm an Engineer" → Login page → Enter `engineer@buildme.demo / demo1234` → Sign In

---

### STEP 3 — ENGINEER DASHBOARD (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "This is the engineer's command center. BuildMe shows exactly what needs attention across all active sites. Here are 6 projects in Tamil Nadu — Coimbatore, Chennai, Trichy, Madurai, Salem. Two projects need immediate attention: Kumar Residence has overdue inspections and open issues."

**Show:**
- Scroll through "Today's Action Queue" — point at red/yellow alerts
- Scroll through "My Projects" — show status badges (Normal, Review Required, Attention)
- Point at "NEED ATTENTION: 2" stat

---

### STEP 4 — PROJECT TRUTH (45 seconds) ⭐ MOST IMPORTANT

**Say:**
> "Project Truth is BuildMe's core innovation. It tells the complete financial story of a construction project."

**Show:** Click "Project Truth" → Select "Kumar Residence"

> "Kumar Residence in Trichy. 2200 sqft. Currently at roofing stage, 60% complete."

**Scroll down to "Budget Story":**

> "The original estimate was 45 lakhs. The current expected cost is 47.3 lakhs. That's a 5% increase. But here's the key — every change is explained."

**Scroll to "Why the budget changed":**

> "Additional excavation for water table — 72 thousand rupees. TMT bar price increase — 85 thousand. Additional bathroom — 1.4 lakhs. Each change has a confidence level and is linked to evidence."

**Show the Evidence Chain:**
> "Plan → Quote → Site → Evidence → Change → Money → Truth. Every linked event strengthens the chain."

---

### STEP 5 — COST INTELLIGENCE (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "Before construction starts, engineers need to understand what a design will cost. BuildMe uses government benchmark data."

**Show:** Click "Cost Intelligence"

> "This is Engine v1.0 — based on CPWD Government Benchmarks from 2019, TN BCCI Index from 2022-2025, and 59 real quotation line items. It's transparent — not a black box."

**Type in an estimate:**
- Location: Coimbatore
- Area: 1800
- Floors: 2
- Click "Calculate Estimate"

> "The engineer gets a planning estimate with full methodology explanation."

---

### STEP 6 — DESIGN-TO-COST SIMULATOR (20 seconds)

**Say:**
> "When the homeowner asks 'what if we add a bathroom?', the engineer can simulate the budget impact before committing."

**Show:** Click "Design-to-Cost" → Select Kumar Residence

> "Add Bathroom — 80K to 1.5 lakhs. Add Floor — 8 to 15 lakhs. Premium Tiles — 60K to 1.2 lakhs. Each change shows cost range, confidence, and timeline impact."

---

### STEP 7 — QUOTATION INTELLIGENCE (15 seconds)

**Say:**
> "BuildMe structures contractor quotations and identifies scope differences — because two quotations at the same price can have very different scopes."

**Show:** Click "Quotation Intel" → Show scope analysis explanation

---

### CLOSING (15 seconds)

**Say:**
> "BuildMe currently has 12 real construction quotations in the system, benchmark estimation using Tamil Nadu BCCI data, and a complete evidence chain from plan to payment. The cost estimation engine passes 79 out of 79 automated tests. This is a working prototype — not a finished product. We're building this to solve a real problem that affects millions of Indian families every year."

**Show:** Return to dashboard — show the full project portfolio view.

## Recording Tips

- **Speed:** Talk at normal pace. Don't rush.
- **Mouse:** Move slowly and deliberately. Point at what you're describing.
- **Timing:** 3-5 minutes maximum. Evaluator attention is short.
- **Honesty:** Say "prototype" not "product". Say "demonstrates" not "proves".
- **Voice:** Record in a quiet room. Use a headset if possible.
- **Resolution:** Record at 1080p minimum.

2. Copy the share link
